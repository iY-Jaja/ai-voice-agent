THE TECHNOLOGY04 / 05
04 · Responsible support

Helpful, without pretending to be the helpdesk.

What AI Innovation does

Offers concise, general troubleshooting; speaks the customer's language; and prepares a short summary to share with official support.

What AI Innovation does not do

It cannot access accounts, check orders, create tickets, issue refunds, or state company policies. It does not request passwords or payment credentials.

Next: connect an approved company knowledge base and human-support workflow, with authentication and privacy safeguards.
